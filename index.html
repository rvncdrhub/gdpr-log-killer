wallet.dat Address Explorer

Extracts public addresses from a Bitcoin Core wallet.dat and shows which ones hold a balance.

🔒 Your file never leaves this page. Parsing happens entirely in your browser using your CPU. Nothing is uploaded to any server. The only data that goes out is the list of public addresses (not keys), sent to a public block explorer to look up balances. You can disconnect from the internet, load the file, then reconnect before looking up balances if you want to verify this.

Drop your wallet.dat here or click to choose a file
